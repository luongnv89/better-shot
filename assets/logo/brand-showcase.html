§ Concept

The mark, explained.

Capture frame Aperture ring Polish spark

The outer rounded square is the capture region — the rectangle you drag on screen. It reads as a window, a viewfinder, a selection.

Inside, an aperture ring centers the mark. It ties the logo to photography and signals precision: the subject is in focus, exactly where it should be.

The diagonal spark in warm amber is the "better." It cuts through the frame like a shutter flash — the moment a raw capture becomes something worth sharing.

Typographically, Better is set in Fraunces roman; Shot in its italic. The italic carries the same diagonal energy as the spark — the wordmark and mark tell the same story.
