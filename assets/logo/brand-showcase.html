Concept

Selection brackets. A neon spark. The moment of capture.

The mark fuses two ideas: the four corner brackets of a screenshot region selection, and a bright four-point spark at the center — the instant the shot is taken and the polish begins.

Brackets

Universal language for "select a region." Visible in every capture tool ever made — instantly readable.

Spark

The energy of the snap, the gleam of a polished result. Four-point geometry mirrors the four brackets.

Neon on Ink

Terminal-green on near-black. A nod to the developer roots of the product, paired with a confident, modern silhouette.
