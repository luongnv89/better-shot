§ 01 — The fix

From bug-report screenshots to something you're proud to ship.

You ship screenshots that look like bug reports.

Raw captures. Harsh white edges. A stray desktop icon in the corner. You paste them into Slack, docs, or a tweet — and they undersell the work.

Fixing them means opening Figma, aligning shadows, fighting with gradients. Ten minutes later, you've lost your train of thought.

Press ⌘⇧2. Drag. Done.

Better Shot captures the region and opens the editor instantly. Pick a background, nudge the shadow, drop an arrow where it matters, and hit ⌘S.

The polished version is on your clipboard before you'd even have Figma open.

Backgrounds that don't look templated

Mesh gradients, wallpapers, solid colors, transparency — or drop in your own image.

Annotations where the eye goes first

Arrows, circles, numbered callouts, text labels. Auto-incrementing badges for walkthroughs.

Shadow & depth, dialed in

Tune corner radius, blur, noise, and padding from 0 (edge-to-edge) to 200px.

Keyboard-driven everything

Save, copy, undo, redo, close. Shortcuts work even when Better Shot is hidden.

Edit any image — not just captures

Drag in a PNG, JPG, or existing screenshot and polish it the same way.

Stays on your machine

No account. No uploads. No telemetry. Screenshots never leave your Mac.

§ 04 — Questions

Things people ask first.

Is it really free?

Yes. BSD-3 licensed, fully open source, no paid tier. Star the repo on GitHub if it saves you time — that's all we ask.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Yes. Separate builds ship for Apple Silicon (M1 through M5) and Intel. Both are on the Releases page.

Will it upload my screenshots anywhere?

Never. Better Shot runs entirely on your Mac. No network calls, no account, no cloud sync, no telemetry.

What about Windows or Linux?

macOS 10.15 or later only, for now. A cross-platform version isn't on the roadmap — the app leans on macOS-native capture APIs.

Can I edit images I didn't capture?

Yes. Drag any PNG or JPG into the editor and apply the same backgrounds, shadows, and annotations.

What permissions does it need?

Only Screen Recording, which macOS requires for any screenshot tool. Grant it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording on first launch.

Stop shipping raw screenshots.

Free forever. Installs in thirty seconds via Homebrew. Works the moment you press the shortcut.
